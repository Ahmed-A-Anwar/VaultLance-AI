# F2 — new freelancer

Demo mode, chromium 1440×900. **Pass: 4 clicks; submit → success card 1.01 s;
drawer opens in 109 ms.**

| Step | Observation |
|---|---|
| `/signup` | role picker → freelancer form; title "Create your account" |
| Fill + submit | button reads "Creating account...", then the card "Your freelancer vault is open." at 1.01 s with the account email |
| "Go to Dashboard" | dashboard loads; first action "New vault" |
| "New vault" | drawer visible in 109 ms |

Friction:
- **1** — the success card does not offer "create your first vault" as its
  primary action; it goes to the dashboard, where the same action is one more
  click. Acceptable, not fixed.
- **1** — no visible account-created confirmation beyond the card (no email
  sent in demo). Expected in demo mode.

Keyboard: the role is chosen with Enter, fields typed in order, Enter on
"Create my vault" submits; once the card appears focus is on its heading, "Your
freelancer vault is open." (fixed — it previously fell to `<body>` when the form
unmounted).

Note: the first run reported this flow as blocked. That was a probe defect — it
waited on the word "already" from the "Already have a vault?" footnote, so it
returned 186 ms after submit and never saw the card. The probe now waits for
`.signup-done-h` or `[role=alert]`. No product change was needed.

axe: 0 serious/critical on the freelancer dashboard.
